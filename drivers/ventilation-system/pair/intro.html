Balanced Ventilation System

A virtual device that controls both of your Roomie fans as one balanced system.

What you get on top of the individual fan devices:

  • One speed slider drives both fans at matching airflow
  • Modes: Away / Normal / Boost / Kitchen — each with its own speed
  • Auto-boost on humidity threshold (with hysteresis)
  • Optional imbalance offset if your house has pressure bias
